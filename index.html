      How to play

      Capture every territory to win, or hold your capital, the Meridian Mine and 60% of the map for two full rounds. Each turn: reinforce (troops = territories ÷ 3, minimum 3, plus continent bonuses, card sets and a +2 catch-up if you are last) on land connected to your capital, spend coin on quality (specialists, forts, cannon), attack (strength-based battles: each round both sides take losses, and a broken defender retreats), then one fortify move within range. Capture a territory to earn a card. Open the Field manual for every rule.
